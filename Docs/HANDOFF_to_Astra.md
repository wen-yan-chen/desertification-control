## What is verified and what is not

Verified by running: raw downloads complete (49 files in manifest); ET0 agrees with two independent sources within 5 %; water balance closes (residual 1e-12 mm); hold-out scores are computed on data never used in fitting.

Not verified, do not present as fact:

- The water–cover curve (e.g. 624 mm/yr to hold 30 % cover) is model output. No field measurement of belt cover against applied water is in the repo. Quote it as the range 400–750 mm/yr.
- `m_w` (drought mortality) and `kcb_full` are assumptions and drive most of that range.
- Converted pixels are irrigated land of unknown type, possibly orchards or fields.
- The model has no skill for year-to-year change of natural cover (hold-out R² = −0.76, same as predicting the mean).
- Fryberger 1979 and Gutman & Ignatov 1998 are cited from memory; the originals are not in `Reference/`.

## Decisions the team should confirm

1. Section: Yutian–Minfeng, 100 km × 1.5 km (assumption A1). Changing it means editing `m1_config.py` and re-running all three scripts (about 40 minutes of MODIS download).
2. Critical cover `v_c = 0.30` from Yizhaq & Ashkenazy. Many field studies use 15–20 %. It is the natural policy threshold for Task 2, so the team should choose it deliberately.
3. Drift potential from ERA5 hourly mean wind (about 50 VU). If a station DP for Minfeng or Yutian can be found, scale to it with `load_forcing(scale={"DP": k})`.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Pin down `kcb_full` and `m_w`.** Look for measured sap flow or ET of Haloxylon/Tamarix/Calligonum under drip irrigation, and for survival after irrigation was cut. Candidates already in `Reference/`: `02_灌溉间隔.pdf` (irrigation-interval experiment with growth and survival), `06_经济效益.pdf`, and the CAJ files on ecological water demand. Each number needs page, species, site and unit.
2. **Check land use of the converted windows** (MODIS MCD12Q1 through the same ORNL API, or Sentinel-2 by eye). If CON1–CON3 are cropland, refit `r`, `V_max` on shrub pixels only.
3. **Task 2 link.** Use `simulate()` with cohorts: each year's newly treated area is its own run started in that year with `v0 ≈ 0.05` and a yearly quota `{year: mm}`; area-weight the cohorts. Water constraint = sum over cohorts of quota × area ≤ available volume. `Params.shelter` is the hook for straw checkerboards. Do not replace the model by a fitted `V(A)` curve as the old `dp_plan` did.
4. **Task 3 link.** Sample storms from the daily DP series in `forcing_*.csv`, droughts through `scale={"ET0":…, "P":…}` or by cutting the quota, and trends from `Results/model1/annual_drivers.csv`. Recovery time is computable from `simulate()` output.
5. **Paper.** Section 3 of the doc has the equations in order; the parameter table has sources. Fill `Paper/main.tex` §Model 1 from it and add FAO-56, Zhang 2016, Liu 2022, Yizhaq & Ashkenazy 2021, Fécan 1999 to `Paper/bibliography/references.bib`.

## Rules I followed and suggest keeping

- Fit only on calibration data; report hold-out next to a naive baseline; keep negative R².
- Every parameter is one of: tabulated source with page, calibrated here, or a numbered assumption with a range in the sensitivity run.
- A failed download stops the script. Nothing is interpolated or filled.
- Request the reanalysis model explicitly (`era5_seamless`). The default changes source in 2017.
